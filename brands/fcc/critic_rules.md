# FCC critic rules (Flipkart Commerce Cloud)
Source: source/FCC_rules.md, corrected against past/ (past work wins). Overrides skill/critic_rules.md.
Allowed despite base rules: three-column grid, stat callout grid, pill headers, numbered markers (01, 02...).

## Locked (any FAIL = revise)
F1. Canvas 1920px wide, height fits content, background #FFFFFF.
F2. Header bar at top: #0164F0 at 10% opacity, 118px tall, title only.
F3. Title: Inter Medium 500, 48px, #0164F0, centred, one line.
F4. Inter is the only font on the canvas.
F5. Body text #383838; never grey or any other ink.
F6. Only these colours: #0164F0, #4E93F5, #2872ED, #E6F0FE, #383838, #FFFFFF, #F2F2F2, #888888 (outlines).
F7. Content is a centred block <= 1500px wide with >= 80px padding; side margins equal.

## Typography
F8. Item headings Inter Medium ~32px; body Inter Regular ~30px; never all caps.
F9. Text on #0164F0 / #4E93F5 fills is #FFFFFF; text on #E6F0FE is #383838 or #0164F0.
F10. Each item: short heading + at most one or two lines of body.

## Components
F11. No drop shadows, gradients or emoji. Depth = tint fill or thin outline only.
F12. Cards: radius ~20px; tint fill or white with 3px #4E93F5 outline.
F13. Pills / card header bars: #0164F0 or #4E93F5 fill, white text, radius ~20px.
F14. Markers: two-digit numbers (01, 02...) in #4E93F5 inside a 3px circle or hexagon, or a check.
F15. Icons: line style, recoloured #4E93F5, one stroke weight; size scales with the item (~0.6 x item height).
F16. Connectors: thin #4E93F5 lines, dashed or solid, aligned to marker centres.
F17. Layout family is in the FCC list and differs from the last approved FCC infographic.

## Tables (cell_table)
F18. Separate rounded cells with even gaps, no full-width divider lines.
F19. Header cells #0164F0 with white SemiBold text; label column #E6F0FE or #F2F2F2; data cells #F2F2F2.
F20. Cell text centred, ink #383838, ~28-30px.
